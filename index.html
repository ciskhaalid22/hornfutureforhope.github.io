Institutional Overview

Horn Future for Hope (HFH) is a non-governmental, non-profit organization headquartered in Hargeisa, Somaliland. We combine operational field implementation with a dedicated Social Research and MERL unit to deliver data-driven humanitarian solutions across public health, climate change adaptation, WASH, education, and child protection.

Our work focuses heavily on mitigating recurrent climate shocks, supporting internally displaced persons (IDPs) and vulnerable pastoralist communities, and fostering long-term socio-economic equity.

Core Programmatic Domains

Public Health & Nutrition

Rehabilitating primary health posts, managing community-based malnutrition treatment, and executing epidemic surveillance in rural areas.

Climate Change & WASH

Building solar-powered deep boreholes, rainwater harvesting catchments, drought-resistant pastoral systems, and community sanitation grids.

Education & Protection

Constructing inclusive resilient schools, guaranteeing zero-cost enrolment for displaced youth, and safeguarding women and children against GBV.

Flagship Grant Project (2026)

Construction & Operation of a Climate-Resilient School in Maroodi Jeex

Total Grant Budget: USD $932,074.00

Target Beneficiaries: 600 Underprivileged, Orphan, and Displaced Children; 3,500 Community Members.

This 18-month institutional project delivers 18 solar-powered climate-resilient classrooms, student dormitories, a computer ICT laboratory, gender-segregated WASH facilities, and daily school feeding to break generational poverty cycles in IDP settlements around Hargeisa.
